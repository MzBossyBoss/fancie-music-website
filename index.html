THE ARTIST BEHIND THE LABEL

FANCIE'S PROFILE

FANCIE is the recording artist behind FANCIE MUSIC, the independent record label. Alongside music, FANCIE brings a technical background and a lifelong interest in technology.

I started writing code when I was 10 years old and loved it ever since. My favorite programming languages are Python, C#, Java, Perl, and C++.

BEYOND THE MUSIC

FANCIE'S SKILLS

Database Design

Databases are so much fun to design and build. I've been working with databases for 20 years from traditional relational databases to NoSQL. I've loved designing databases for different needs.

Artificial Intelligence

I'm interested in what we can do with AI in the future.

Bioinformatics

My research area is bioinformatics. I created a Java program to detect remote protein homologs and thereby determine an unknown protein's function.
